ARTIST STATEMENT

Jinhao Wang (b. 2003) graduated from the Oil Painting Department of Beijing Institute of Fashion Technology and currently lives and works in Beijing.

His practice involves painting and digital art, exploring structural violence within social relationships, particularly the effects of patriarchal society on individuals.

Through a direct and unembellished approach to painting, Wang depicts figures that evoke a sense of fear, grotesqueness, and alienation.

His recent digital collage series uses computer-generated imagery, searching for images within pornography-related videos and political news, which are then fragmented and recombined into absurd and visually stimulating works.

艺术家自述

王锦皓（2003—），毕业于北京服装学院油画系， 现工作生活于北京。

创作涉及绘画和数字艺术，探讨社会关系中的结构性暴力， 尤其关注父权社会对个体的影响。

他通过直接的绘画方式不加修饰地去表现， 画面里的人物给人以恐怖、怪诞与异化的感受。

近年的数字拼贴系列使用电脑制图， 在色情视频和政治新闻中搜寻图片， 将其拆分并重新组合， 制作荒诞而又充满视觉刺激的图像作品。
